The Creator Cheat Sheet

Hook formulas, how the algorithm actually decides what to push, and the content system that separates consistent creators from one-hit-wonders. One page, no fluff.

1. Hook Formulas

The Gap
Open with a piece of information the viewer doesn't have yet, and make it impossible to scroll past without knowing it.
"The reason [common belief] is wrong..."
The Callout
Name the exact person watching in your first three words. Specificity stops the scroll faster than a general statement ever will.
"If you're a [specific person], watch this."
The Contradiction
State something that sounds backwards on the surface. Curiosity about *how that's possible* is what holds attention.
"I [did the opposite thing] and it worked better."

2. How the Algorithm Actually Decides

  • Watch time beats everything else early on. The first few seconds determine whether your video gets shown to more people at all — this is why the hook matters more than the rest of the video combined.
  • Re-watches are a stronger signal than likes. A short video someone watches twice tells the platform more than ten likes ever will.
  • Consistency in posting matters less than consistency in topic. The algorithm is trying to understand who your audience is — switching topics constantly confuses that signal more than posting less often does.
  • Comments that start a reply chain outperform comment count alone. A controversial-but-fair opinion that gets people debating in your comments is worth more than ten "love this" comments.

3. The Content System

Step 1 — Pillars, not topics
Pick 3 to 4 recurring pillars you'll always come back to, not a fresh topic every video. This is what lets an algorithm (and a human audience) understand what to expect from you.
Step 2 — Batch the hooks first
Write 10 hooks before you script a single full video. Most creators run out of ideas because they try to write hook and content at the same time, every single time.
Step 3 — One clear takeaway, always
If someone can't repeat your video's point in one sentence after watching it, it didn't have one. Cut anything that doesn't serve that single takeaway.
