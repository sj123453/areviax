No Purchase Necessary

Entry to this draw is free. Leaving a review is one way to enter, but is never required — the Free Entry option above offers exactly the same odds of winning with no review, purchase, or payment of any kind.

How Winners Are Chosen

One winner is selected entirely at random from all valid entries (review submissions and free entries combined, weighted equally) received during the calendar month. The draw is not based on review content, star rating, or product purchased.

The Prize

The winner may choose any one Areviax digital product or one month of any Areviax subscription, provided free of charge. The prize is non-transferable and has no cash alternative.

Eligibility

Open to entrants aged 16 or over. One entry per person per month, regardless of how many reviews or entries are submitted. Areviax staff and their immediate family are not eligible.

Winner Notification

The winner is contacted by the email address provided within 7 days of the month's end. If unclaimed after 14 days, a new winner is drawn.

Data

Your email is used only to run this draw and contact you if you win. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
