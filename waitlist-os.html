Questions

Good things to ask a Titan.

Is it free to join?

Yes — free, no card, no deposit. You’re just getting in line for early access.

What’s OS actually for?

Everything outside the gym: study blocks, habits, goals and your weekly schedule, wrapped in one compass ring so you can see the whole day at once.

Do I need Areviax Mass?

No. OS stands on its own. If you run both, streaks and rewards will be shared across them.

When does it launch?

It’s live in testing now, with a founder feedback round next. The roadmap above is the real order.

Will it work on my phone?

Yes — it’s a progressive web app. Add it to your home screen and it behaves like a native app.
