Questions

Good things to ask a Titan.

Is it free to join the waitlist?

Yes — free, no card, no obligation. You only pay if you choose to reserve the Founder+ rate, and even then it’s a payment link we email you later, not an instant charge.

When does it launch?

It’s live in testing now, with a founder feedback round next, then public launch. The roadmap above is the real order — we’d rather ship it right than ship it fast.

What does “locked for life” mean?

Your founder rate stays the same for as long as your subscription stays active, even after Standard pricing changes.

Is it for beginners or experienced lifters?

Both. Realistic goals, rescue workouts for bad days, and a rank system that rewards consistency over ego lifts.

Does it work offline / on my phone?

It’s a progressive web app — add it to your home screen and it runs like a native app, including offline for core features.

What’s the difference between Mass and OS?

Mass is the fitness and nutrition app. Areviax OS is the command centre for everything else — study, habits, goals and schedule. They’ll share streaks and rewards if you run both.
